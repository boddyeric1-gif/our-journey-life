## Full description

> Our Journey is a private sanctuary built for two.
>
> Each day you each answer a single prompt. Neither of you sees the other's answer until both have written. Small daily rituals that hold a relationship up.
>
> Around the daily Spark sit two deeper tools:
> - **Argument Aftercare** — a structured, calm bridge out of the freeze after conflict.
> - **Sealed Time Capsules** — letters and memories that unlock later, building shared history that becomes more valuable over time.
>
> Multi-day quests on listening, trust, and repair. No quizzes. No leaderboards. No scoring you against other couples.
>
> One subscription covers both partners. Built for privacy — your writing stays yours.
>
> Designed for couples in any season: dating, engaged, married, long-distance, or long-term.
