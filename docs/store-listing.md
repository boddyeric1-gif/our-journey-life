## Full description

> Our Journey is a quietly made app for two.
>
> Each day you each answer a single prompt — a question about gratitude, memory, curiosity, or tenderness. Neither of you sees the other's answer until you've both written. It's a small ritual that has a way of holding a relationship up.
>
> Around the daily Spark, there are gentle, multi-day **quests** on the things that matter most: listening without fixing, repair after a small rupture, the soft start of a hard conversation, the architecture of trust. No quizzes. No scoring you against another couple. Just one small act at a time.
>
> When your partner is offline, the app is still meaningful: solo reflections, private letters you can send to the future, short research-backed insights from the people who have spent careers studying love.
>
> What's inside:
> - A daily Spark, with dual-reveal so neither of you is reading ahead
> - Twelve quest chapters on communication and trust, with more on the way
> - Letters you write and your partner reads
> - Streaks and quiet milestones that respect that some weeks are harder
> - Solo mode for the days you're not in sync
> - Designed for couples in any season — dating, engaged, married, long-term
>
> Built for privacy. Your writing is yours and your partner's — nobody else's. We never sell or train on it.
